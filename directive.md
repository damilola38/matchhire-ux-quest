# Directive

## Objective
Help new job seekers reach open roles in 2 clicks, without an account prompt, by improving the navigation of the MatchHire site.

## Scope
**In scope:**
- Separate "Job Seeker" and "Employer" entry points in the top navigation
- A new "Job Openings" link that leads directly to open roles
- A "Browse and apply without creating an account" note
- Restoring the "How it works" section on the Job Seeker page
- Loading and mobile states for the changed screens

**Out of scope:**
- Renaming or changing what "Must Hunt" does (it stays as it is)
- Employer features, pricing, and the application flow
- Visual rebrand or new pages

## Requirements
1. "Job Seeker" and "Employer" are clearly labeled and lead to their own screens.
2. "Job Openings" and "Must Hunt" both appear as separate links.
3. Open roles are reachable in 2 clicks from the home page.
4. No sign-up wall before roles are shown.
5. All screens keep the existing site's colors, fonts, and layout.
6. Each changed screen has empty, loading, error, and mobile versions.

## Completion criteria
- Problem map, evidence report, and redesign screens are complete.
- Prototype shows the 2-click path from start to open roles.
- Success metric test is run, or its plan is documented with a clear method.
- AI use is documented in the process log.
