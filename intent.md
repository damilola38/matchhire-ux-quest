# Intent: why I picked this problem

## The problem I chose
New job seekers can't find open roles from the main navigation (P01).

## Why this one
- **It blocks everything after it.** If a visitor can't find roles, they never reach applying or submitting, so every later problem is irrelevant to them.
- **I saw it happen.** 3 of 3 testers failed the same way, so this is observed behavior, not just my opinion.
- **The fix is small and contained.** It only needs a navigation change. It doesn't need new features or a new product.
- **It is the entry point.** It affects every new job seeker who doesn't arrive via a direct application link.

## Why not the others
- [Save and continue button ambiguous]: affects people who are already inside the flow, so fewer people hit it.
- [Pricing page audience unclear]: affects a narrower group and doesn't stop someone from reaching the core job.
- [Landing pages look similar]: partly covered by this fix, since clear Job Seeker and Employer entry points address it.

## What I'm unsure about
Small sample of 3 people from my contacts. I'm confident the problem exists, but not about how often it happens across all visitors.
